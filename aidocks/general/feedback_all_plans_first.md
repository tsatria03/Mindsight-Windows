**Write all the plans before reviewing any of them** (the dev, 2026-10-22: "Please write down the other plans as well if possible. I do not want to review any sections for a plan untill all of them are written with the answers I give from your questions. One plan at a time.").

**Why:** the dev wants the whole port settled on paper, every question answered, before any building starts, so a later answer cannot undo an earlier plan's review.

**How to apply:**
- Write the plans in order, one at a time. A plan is written when its note is committed and every one of its open questions has the dev's answer in its Decided section.
- Ask its questions one per reply ([[feedback_one_question_at_a_time]]), recording each answer in the plan, and committing, before the next question.
- Bring in every item from [[project_later_topics]] that belongs to the plan, and strike it from there.
- Only when the last plan is written, offer plan 1's section review ([[feedback_review_plan_sections]]). Nothing is built before then.
- If a later plan's answer changes an earlier plan, update the earlier note too, and say so.
