##Finished.

Swiping left on the credits opens the creator's dissertation on the game.
The game saves your place at the story's checkpoints, and Continue on the main menu carries on from it.
The whole game can be played, from its main menu, through every puzzle, to its ending and credits.
A short buzz plays where the original vibrated.
Pausing works with Escape, Enter or P, and switching to another window pauses the game by itself.
